# Code of Conduct

## The standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc]. The full text
is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and
governs all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and private
correspondence about the project.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The short version: behave so that people are willing to keep working with you.
Be respectful of differing viewpoints, accept correction gracefully, and give it
the same way. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not tolerated in any form.

## One project-specific note

Most of the traffic here is people saying that something in a skill is wrong.
That is the point of the project, and it is welcome — a report that a shipped
code sample does not compile is a gift, not an attack. Report the defect, not
the person who wrote it, and expect the same in return.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through the contact form at
<https://aktagon.com>. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's
identity is not disclosed.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder: a private
correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, chosen to match the
severity and the pattern. Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith are
subject to it themselves.
